Pancake
-----Ingredients----
Flour,Sugar,egg,milk,Baking Soda,Butter
-----Cooking Methods----
1.Mix all dry Ingredients(Flour,Sugar,Baking Soda)
2.Add luke warm milk and melted Butter
3.Heat the Pan
4.Pour the batter on the heating Pan
5.Wait till the batter is cooked
6.Flip the pan cake 
7.Serve the hot pancake with honey
